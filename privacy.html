🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number
  • Fitness Data: Weight, height, age, gender, fitness goals
  • Workout History: Exercise logs, session attendance, performance metrics
  • Progress Photos: Images you upload to track your transformation
  • Payment Information: Purchase history for session coins
  • Communication: Messages with your coach

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide personalized fitness coaching and training plans
  • Track your progress and workout history
  • Enable communication with your assigned coach
  • Process payments for premium features
  • Send notifications about sessions and updates
  • Improve our AI-powered recommendations
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard practices:

  • Encryption: All sensitive data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Authentication: JWT tokens for secure session management
  • Database Security: Supabase provides enterprise-grade security
  • Regular Audits: We review security measures regularly

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Your information is only shared with:

  • Your Coach: To provide personalized training services
  • Payment Processors: For secure transaction processing
  • Service Providers: Cloud hosting, analytics (anonymized data)
  • Legal Authorities: When required by applicable law

5. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Permanently delete your account and all data
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications
  • Data Portability: Request your data in a portable format

6. Contact Us

For any privacy concerns or questions, contact us:

  • Email: support@vigora.com
  • Response Time: Within 48 hours
