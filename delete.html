🗑️ Delete Account

Last Updated: July 2026

⚠️ This action is permanent!

Deleting your account will permanently remove all your data. This cannot be undone.

What happens when you delete your account?

  • ✅ Your profile information will be permanently removed
  • ✅ Your progress photos and history will be deleted
  • ✅ Your chat history with coaches will be lost
  • ✅ Any remaining coins will be forfeited
  • ✅ Your account will no longer be accessible
  • ✅ Your data will be removed from our systems

How to delete your account

1

Open the Vigora App

Launch the Vigora app on your device

2

Go to Profile

Tap on the profile icon in the bottom navigation

3

Select "Delete Account"

Scroll down and tap on "Delete Account"

4

Confirm Deletion

Read the confirmation message and confirm your decision

Before you delete your account

  • Download your data: You can request a copy of your data before deletion
  • Use remaining coins: Spend any remaining coins or they will be lost
  • Contact support: Email us if you need help

Need help?

If you have questions about account deletion, contact our support team:

  • Email: support@vigora.com
  • Response Time: Within 24 hours
